About

I’m Zeeshan Ahmad, an illustrator working in moody, narrative imagery for books and editorials. My work leans into atmosphere, shadow, texture, and the quiet tension between figure and space.

I have been making illustrations professionally since 2022. My practice sits at the intersection of fantasy, folklore, and literary fiction.
